## 1. Purpose

PR2-FICT defines how fiction and LitRPG may contribute experience,
world-behavior, capability, interaction, and evaluation pressure to Myravant
without becoming presumptive Myravant content inputs, implementation
specifications, gameplay doctrine, or hidden metaphysical law.

A fictional work demonstrates what an author or narrative can depict. It does
not by itself demonstrate that a real implementation is feasible, performant,
desirable, safe, balanced, maintainable, socially acceptable, or preferred by
players.

PR2-FICT governs interpretation only. It does not authorize source acquisition,
source processing, broad corpus research, Myravant-native content authoring,
canon promotion, runtime implementation, model training, conversion, or
live-play behavior.

## 2. Authority boundary

PR2-FICT owns only:

- fiction/LitRPG-specific interpretation rules;
- the distinction between source-local depiction and research pressure;
- fiction-specific overclaim and narrative-convenience checks;
- experience-pressure extraction rules;
- world-behavior and capability-pressure extraction rules;
- fiction-derived evaluation-scenario candidate rules;
- preservation of uncertainty;
- fiction-specific handoff and escalation.

PR2-FICT consumes the source-research architecture and method qualified by
PR2-SRC.

PR2-FICT coordinates with, but does not replace:

- PR2-CORPUS for registry, batching, genealogy, coverage, novelty, saturation,
  outlier, skew, and bias governance;
- PR2-ORG for originality, rights, similarity, contamination, quarantine, and
  distribution eligibility;
- PR2-IR for the source-analysis / Myravant-design information barrier.

PR2-FICT must not own source acquisition, research execution, corpus selection,
originality or rights adjudication, information-barrier policy, simulation
exemplar interpretation, Myravant-native content authorship, gameplay doctrine,
Myravant metaphysics, canon promotion, runtime architecture, production schemas,
model training, conversion execution, or live-play/GM behavior.

If fiction-specific interpretation would require taking one of those
authorities, the lawful result is handoff or escalation.

## 3. Relationship to the source-research pipeline

PR2-SRC establishes:

```text
external evidence
-> source-aware observation
-> normalized pressure
-> cross-source synthesis
-> Myravant-facing requirement
-> separately governed independent design
```

PR2-FICT does not replace or bypass that pipeline.

It qualifies one narrow question: when the external evidence is fictional or
strongly narrative, what may research infer from the depiction, and what must
remain source-local, uncertain, or prohibited?

PR2-FICT ends before independent Myravant design begins.

## 4. Central laws

### 4.1 Fiction is pressure evidence, not implementation evidence

A fictional depiction may reveal an experience or capability worth testing. It
does not establish implementation feasibility, runtime cost, performance,
security, maintainability, operational reliability, accessibility, moderation
requirements, or deployment architecture.

### 4.2 Source-local depiction is not Myravant law

A depicted world rule, cosmology, progression structure, resource model,
technology, institution, body model, afterlife, economy, social convention, or
power system remains source-local evidence.

It does not become Myravant doctrine by being coherent, popular, emotionally
effective, frequently repeated, or central to the source.

### 4.3 A plot event is not automatically a stable system rule

Narratives may contain exceptions, dramatic coincidences, authorial
intervention, selective causality, unreliable explanations, retcons, or
one-time events.

A single event must not be generalized into a reusable system law without
additional evidence.

### 4.4 Protagonist capability is not player baseline

Exceptional access, destiny, privilege, rare powers, unique equipment,
narrative protection, unusual knowledge, exceptional social position, or
protagonist survivorship must not be interpreted as ordinary player-grade
capability, universal availability, or intended baseline.

### 4.5 Narrative omission is not evidence of absence

Fiction routinely omits maintenance, logistics, downtime, childcare, sanitation,
administration, moderation, accessibility, training, recovery, boring work,
support infrastructure, ordinary actors, and other costs or processes.

What is not shown must not silently be treated as unnecessary.

### 4.6 Narrative desirability is not measured user preference

A source may portray an experience as exciting, tragic, satisfying, frightening,
romantic, funny, oppressive, meaningful, or desirable. That framing is evidence
about the narrative, not empirical evidence that real users want the same
experience or accept the same risks.

### 4.7 Narrative possibility is not technical feasibility

A story can compress time, omit computation, handwave scale, grant perfect
information, or ignore coordination cost. A fiction-derived pressure may ask
Myravant to support an experience or world behavior. It may not claim that a
particular architecture can deliver it.

### 4.8 Familiarity and popularity grant no authority

Fame, sales, adaptation count, fandom size, cultural prominence, citation
frequency, recommendation frequency, or personal familiarity do not make a
fiction source more authoritative. Corpus weighting remains governed by
PR2-CORPUS.

### 4.9 Abstraction is not permission to copy

Renaming characters, paraphrasing plots, changing numbers, translating
terminology, merging several fictional sources, or using AI rewriting does not
create independent Myravant content. PR2-ORG and PR2-IR remain controlling
authorities for eligibility and information flow.

### 4.10 Fiction-derived pressure remains falsifiable

A useful pressure must be expressible in a way that can later be tested,
qualified, contradicted, narrowed, or rejected. “Make Myravant feel like this
story” is not a lawful requirement.

## 5. Fiction-specific observation classes

PR2-FICT requires fiction research to distinguish, when materially relevant:

- depicted behavior;
- diegetic rule claim;
- character belief or report;
- narrator or exposition claim;
- narrative consequence;
- researcher inference.

These are interpretation aids, not a universal literary ontology.

Character belief must not be silently upgraded to source-local fact.
Researcher inference must remain marked as inference and carry uncertainty.

## 6. Fictionalization-risk flags

A fiction-derived claim must preserve material uncertainty created by narrative
construction. Relevant flags may include:

- protagonist exceptionalism;
- protagonist survivorship;
- selective viewpoint;
- unreliable perspective;
- narrative time compression;
- montage or skipped-process compression;
- offscreen maintenance or support;
- perfect-information convenience;
- causality simplification;
- coincidence or plot-dependent resolution;
- rule inconsistency;
- continuity revision or retcon;
- scale handwave;
- resource or logistics omission;
- social-response omission;
- institutional-response omission;
- genre convention;
- symbolic or metaphorical treatment;
- adaptation or shared-lineage repetition;
- source-local exception presented without general rule.

A flag does not invalidate the evidence. It constrains what may be inferred.

## 7. Experience-pressure extraction

Fiction may lawfully pressure Myravant on experiences that are difficult to
express as isolated mechanics, including:

- meaningful freedom of action;
- persistent consequence;
- continuity across sessions, locations, bodies, institutions, and long time
  horizons;
- world reactivity;
- discovery and uncertainty;
- emergent narrative;
- systemic depth;
- noncombat professions and ordinary work;
- social continuity;
- attachment and companionship;
- family and long-term relationships;
- romance-capable interaction;
- humor and comedy;
- identity and embodiment;
- reputation and social memory;
- economies and material interdependence;
- politics, factions, governance, and institutions;
- player-created organizations;
- maintenance, recovery, aging, and lifecycle;
- coexistence of exceptional and ordinary actors;
- meaningful consequences for choices outside combat;
- world-scale events that do not erase local agency.

These are pressure families, not promised features.

## 8. World-behavior and capability pressure

A fiction-derived world-behavior pressure asks what the world would need to be
capable of doing for an observed experience to remain coherent.

Examples include persistent relationship consequence, institutional memory,
economic dependency, local world reactivity, meaningful information asymmetry,
ordinary-life processes, and continuity across embodiment changes, travel,
absence, or long time horizons.

The answer is not supplied by the fiction. The fiction reveals the pressure.

A source-local depiction may become a normalized fiction pressure only when the
record can state the pressure without requiring the source's protected or
signature expression.

A lawful normalized pressure should be capable of carrying at least:

```text

```

This is a governance contract, not a production schema.

A pressure statement asks what capability, constraint, interaction, failure
mode, or experience a future Myravant design owner must consider. It does not
answer what source content Myravant should recreate.

## 10. Content non-transfer law

The following source-local material must never become a normal PR2-FICT output:

- characters;
- character biographies;
- character relationships as reusable content;
- plot events or plot arcs;
- settings;
- locations;
- factions;
- named institutions;
- cultures;
- species or ancestries;
- signature terminology;
- signature power systems;
- signature progression structures;
- signature items;
- signature monsters or creatures;
- signature technologies;
- signature cosmologies;
- recognizable combinations whose identity depends on the source;
- dialogue;
- prose;
- scene structure intended for recreation.

Such material may exist inside a lawful segregated research plane where needed
for audit. It is not a Myravant-facing deliverable.

## 11. Evaluation-scenario candidates

PR2-FICT may produce an evaluation-scenario candidate when the scenario is
expressed independently of source identity and tests a general capability,
failure mode, or interaction pressure.

A candidate should be capable of carrying:

```text
evaluation_scenario_candidate_id
pressure_refs[]
experience_condition
world_behavior_condition
actor_classes[]
relevant_scale
relevant_time_horizon
required_state_or_memory
interaction_dependencies[]
observable_success_conditions[]
observable_failure_conditions[]
preserved_uncertainties[]
source_independence_check
handoff_state
```

An evaluation-scenario candidate is not:

- a recreated scene;
- an adventure;
- a quest;
- a plot outline;
- a character simulation;
- canonical content;
- live-play material.

The lawful value is the interaction pressure. A source-specific scene does not
cross the boundary.

## 12. Fiction-derived anti-pattern pressure

Fiction may expose failure hypotheses such as worlds that only react around
protagonists, cosmetic professions, price-only economies, relationships that
reset when scenes end, institutions with no memory, scale with no local
consequence, persistence with no correction path, or simulated societies that
are merely decorative.

PR2-FICT may normalize these into failure hypotheses or evaluation conditions.
It may not assume that narrative criticism automatically proves a real design
failure.

## 13. Counterpressure and tradeoff preservation

PR2-FICT must preserve material tensions rather than flatten them, including:

- freedom versus consequence;
- persistence versus correction;
- deep simulation versus comprehensibility;
- scale versus local fidelity;
- privacy versus social memory;
- discovery versus player legibility;
- autonomy versus safety;
- permanence versus experimentation;
- economic depth versus accessibility;
- social continuity versus user control;
- realism versus pacing;
- ordinary-life depth versus cognitive load.

A fiction-derived requirement that erases the cost side of an experience is
incomplete.

## 14. Cross-source synthesis and lineage

PR2-FICT may participate in cross-source synthesis only through the PR2-SRC
pipeline.

Cross-source agreement does not create doctrine. Cross-source disagreement is
not noise. A single high-information outlier may expose a project-falsifying
requirement.

Adaptations, franchises, shared story lineage, genre convention, or direct
influence may repeat the same pressure. PR2-FICT may record
interpretation-relevant lineage signals, but PR2-CORPUS owns genealogy and
effective-independence accounting.

## 15. Actual-play boundary

Actual play can contain both fictional narrative and evidence about real player
behavior.

PR2-FICT owns only the fiction/narrative interpretation component. Claims about
what real participants did, preferred, misunderstood, exploited, or experienced
belong under the applicable PR2-SRC evidence mode.

Edited or dramatized presentation must not be treated as direct behavioral
evidence merely because real participants appear in it.

## 16. Feasibility and implementation handoff

When a fiction pressure implies difficult technical or operational questions,
PR2-FICT records the pressure and hands feasibility outward.

It must not infer runtime topology, database choice, networking model,
simulation frequency, AI architecture, concurrency model, persistence
mechanism, rendering architecture, moderation implementation, cost model, or
hardware requirement.

## 17. Relationship to PR2-ORG

PR2-FICT does not determine that a normalized pressure is original, legally
safe, or distribution-eligible.

If a later candidate appears source-shaped, recognizable, or materially
dependent on protected expression, PR2-ORG controls the eligibility response.

No fiction-specific interpretation rule can waive PR2-ORG review.

## 18. Relationship to PR2-IR

Source-local fiction detail belongs in the source-aware research plane. The
ordinary Myravant design context should receive only the lawful abstraction
required for independent design.

PR2-FICT does not redefine the IR handoff payload or exposure policy.

If a fiction pressure cannot be expressed without unnecessary source-shaped
detail, the lawful result is an applicable PR2-IR blocked or escalated
disposition, not a weaker paraphrase.

## 19. Relationship to PR2-CORPUS

PR2-CORPUS decides how fiction fits into the 1,000+ source research portfolio.

PR2-FICT does not determine how many fiction sources are required, genre quotas,
which works receive deep analysis, whether a region is saturated, whether one
narrative lineage counts as independent evidence, or whether fiction is
overrepresented or underrepresented.

PR2-FICT interprets selected fiction evidence. PR2-CORPUS governs portfolio
composition.

## 20. Lawful PR2-FICT output classes

A fiction research unit must route to one or more of:

- `experience_pressure`;
- `system_pressure`;
- `world_behavior_pressure`;
- `interaction_pressure`;
- `evaluation_scenario_candidate`;
- `counterpressure_or_tradeoff`;
- `outlier_pressure`;
- `uncertainty_requires_more_research`;
- `handoff_to_existing_owner`;
- `escalated_missing_doctrine`;
- `rejected_invalid_inference`.

They are research outcomes. They are not Myravant content categories.

For compatibility with the controlling manifest completion condition,
`world_behavior_pressure` and `interaction_pressure` are specialized
`system_pressure` routes. `counterpressure_or_tradeoff` and `outlier_pressure`
qualify experience or system pressure rather than creating additional
downstream requirement classes. `uncertainty_requires_more_research`,
`handoff_to_existing_owner`, `escalated_missing_doctrine`, and
`rejected_invalid_inference` are governance dispositions.

Substantive fiction-derived observations therefore still route only to
experience requirements, system pressures, or evaluation-scenario candidates.

## 21. Invalid inference patterns

PR2-FICT rejects:

```text
this story depicts it -> Myravant must implement it literally
the protagonist can do it -> every player should be able to do it
the story omits the cost -> the cost does not matter
many stories use the trope -> the trope is Myravant doctrine
the fictional system says it scales -> the architecture is technically scalable
the narrative frames it positively -> users will prefer it
the setting needs this metaphysics -> Myravant needs the same metaphysics
the names were removed -> the content is independently Myravant
the source can be summarized -> the summary is safe design input
```

## 22. Corpus-scale fiction-family pressure

PR2-FICT must remain useful across fantasy, science fiction, science-fantasy,
cultivation, game-lit/LitRPG, horror, investigation, military, political,
institutional, economic, survival, relationship-centered, romance, comedy,
slice-of-life, AI, cybernetic, biotech, vehicle/platform, companion/bonded,
alternate-body, distributed-identity, and long-horizon civilization fiction.

No family is a privileged template for Myravant. Genre conventions are evidence
constraints, not default world law.

## 23. Outliers and project-falsifying pressure

A rare depiction may expose a body-model gap, relationship-persistence problem,
social-memory failure, institutional continuity gap, unusual time-horizon
problem, noncombat profession that current abstractions trivialize, distributed
identity problem, world-state interaction that existing systems cannot express,
or capability that breaks an assumed design boundary.

The lawful response is to preserve the pressure and route it. The response is
not to recreate the source.

## 24. Escalation conditions

PR2-FICT must escalate rather than invent doctrine when:

- a pressure cannot be normalized without retaining source-shaped expression;
- a fictional phenomenon requires a Myravant metaphysical decision;
- a pressure requires a runtime feasibility judgment;
- originality, rights, or similarity adjudication is required;
- an evaluation candidate would become a recognizable recreation;
- source evidence is materially uncertain or contradictory;
- actual-use evidence and dramatized fiction cannot be separated confidently;
- corpus weighting or genealogy is required;
- a new research method is required beyond PR2-SRC;
- a required design owner does not yet exist.

Escalation is a valid output.

## 25. Completion conditions

PR2-FICT is complete only when:

1. fiction is explicitly treated as pressure evidence rather than implementation
   evidence;
2. source-local depiction is prevented from becoming Myravant doctrine;
3. plot events, protagonist privilege, omission, narrative framing, and
   technical handwaving have explicit anti-overclaim treatment;
4. characters, plots, settings, signature terminology, signature power systems,
   items, and recognizable source packages are prohibited outputs;
5. experience, world-behavior, system, interaction, and evaluation pressure have
   lawful source-independent routes;
6. uncertainty and counterpressure are preserved;
7. actual-play behavioral evidence remains distinct from fictional narrative;
8. PR2-SRC, PR2-ORG, PR2-IR, and PR2-CORPUS ownership remains intact;
9. fiction-derived observations route only to experience requirements, system
   pressures, evaluation scenarios, uncertainty, or lawful escalation;
10. no source execution, native content, canon, runtime implementation, model
    training, conversion execution, or live-play authority is created.
