## 1. Purpose

PR2-SRC-A establishes the foundational governance for researching external works
without treating them as presumptive Myravant content.

It replaces the architectural assumption:

`source -> converted Myravant artifact`

with the controlled research posture:

`external evidence -> source-aware observation -> normalized pressure -> cross-source synthesis -> Myravant-facing requirement -> separately governed independent design`

This file governs the research boundary. It does not perform source processing,
define Myravant mechanics, create native content, decide canon, determine
distribution eligibility, or authorize runtime or live-play behavior.

The architecture must survive a heterogeneous external corpus of at least 1,000
sources without requiring one source family, genre, medium, rules tradition,
software architecture, or cultural lineage to become hidden Myravant law.

## 2. What PR2-SRC-A owns

PR2-SRC-A owns only the foundational source-research governance needed before
large-scale research can begin:

- external-source nonauthority;
- source-aware research versus Myravant-design separation at the conceptual level;
- exact internal provenance continuity for research evidence;
- the distinction among source expression, observation, pressure, synthesis,
  requirement, and later design;
- lawful classes of research output;
- cross-source synthesis as the primary route from research into requirements;
- source-modality neutrality;
- functional and generative abstraction requirements;
- corpus-scale anti-drift rules that apply before PR2-CORPUS exists;
- lawful escalation when research cannot be normalized without invention;
- explicit downstream handoffs to later source-governance workstreams.

PR2-SRC-A may state requirements that later tranches must satisfy. It may not
silently implement those later tranches.

## 3. What PR2-SRC-A must not own

PR2-SRC-A must not own or complete:

- originality, rights, similarity, or distribution-eligibility adjudication
  (`PR2-ORG`);
- the final source-analysis-to-Myravant-design representation or information
  barrier (`PR2-IR`);
- the 1,000+ source registry, coverage accounting, genealogy, bias measurement,
  novelty accounting, or saturation governance (`PR2-CORPUS`);
- fiction/LitRPG-specific research rules (`PR2-FICT`);
- simulation/infrastructure exemplar-specific research rules (`PR2-SIMEX`);
- legacy extraction/conversion surface disposition (`PR2-SRC-C`);
- detailed heterogeneous research-method qualification (`PR2-SRC-B`);
- PR2-SRC completion review (`PR2-SRC-D`);
- source acquisition or bulk source processing;
- donor conversion or source-to-Myravant transformation;
- canon promotion;
- native-content production;
- gameplay doctrine invention;
- runtime or production-schema implementation;
- agent reconnaissance execution;
- model training;
- live-play or GM behavior.

If a research problem cannot be solved without taking one of these authorities,
PR2-SRC-A must hand it off or escalate it.

## 4. Foundational research pipeline

The lawful conceptual pipeline is:

```text
external source / evidence
-> controlled source-aware observation
-> normalized structural / functional pressure
-> cross-source comparison and synthesis
-> Myravant-facing requirement
-> separately governed independent Myravant design
```

Each arrow is a boundary, not a rename operation.

A later stage may consume the outputs of an earlier stage only under its own
authority. Existence at one stage never grants authority at the next.

### 4.1 External source / evidence

An external source is evidence about a possibility space, behavior, structure,
failure, experience, implementation, institution, or system.

It is not Myravant doctrine, canon, mechanics, runtime truth, or content merely
because it was researched.

### 4.2 Source-aware observation

A source-aware observation records what the source actually demonstrates or
claims while retaining exact internal provenance.

Observation must remain distinguishable from Myravant terminology and design.

Observation may preserve source-specific facts inside the segregated research
environment when lawful and necessary for auditability. It does not grant those
facts downstream authority.

### 4.3 Normalized pressure

A normalized pressure states the structural, functional, interaction,
constraint, failure, experiential, or capability demand revealed by evidence.

Pressure should reduce unnecessary dependence on source expression while
remaining traceable to the evidence that produced it.

Pressure is evidence, not doctrine.

### 4.4 Cross-source synthesis

Cross-source synthesis compares pressures across independent evidence rather
than treating one source as a production specification.

Synthesis may identify:

- convergent requirements;
- conflicting pressures;
- outliers;
- missing capability;
- hidden assumptions;
- interaction requirements;
- failure conditions;
- coverage gaps;
- uncertainty that requires further research.

Frequency may affect research priority. Frequency does not vote doctrine into
existence.

### 4.5 Myravant-facing requirement

A Myravant-facing requirement expresses a capability, constraint, interaction,
or evaluation need that survives the research synthesis boundary.

A research-derived requirement is still not gameplay doctrine, canon, runtime
implementation, or native content.

It must be handed to the lawful Myravant design or doctrine owner for any later
design decision.

## 5. Mandatory representation distinctions

The following concepts must remain distinguishable throughout the research
program:

1. **source expression** — literal or source-specific expressive material;
2. **source-aware observation** — attributable statement about what evidence
   demonstrates or claims;
3. **normalized pressure** — abstract structural or functional demand;
4. **synthesis result** — relationship among multiple pressures or evidence
   sources;
5. **Myravant-facing requirement** — research-derived capability or constraint
   presented to a lawful Myravant owner;
6. **Myravant design** — separately authorized native design work.

The project must never treat movement between these layers as merely a wording
change.

In particular:

- paraphrase does not create independence;
- terminology replacement does not create independence;
- numeric adjustment does not create independence;
- format conversion does not create independence;
- combining multiple source expressions does not automatically create
  independent Myravant design.

PR2-ORG and PR2-IR will later govern originality/eligibility and information
flow in greater detail.

## 6. Source modality neutrality

PR2-SRC must not assume that a TTRPG book is the universal research unit or that
all evidence should receive the same analysis method.

Lawful evidence may include, when separately acquired and lawfully researched:

- rules-heavy game material;
- content-heavy catalogs and bestiaries;
- generator-heavy tables and procedural systems;
- toolkits;
- settings and adventures;
- solo or GM-less systems;
- professions and occupations;
- fiction and actual play;
- empirical research;
- ordinary-life institutions and practices;
- software documentation and implementations;
- repositories, issue histories, postmortems, and failure reports;
- simulation and infrastructure systems;
- economic, ecological, medical, legal, historical, organizational, social, or
  technical evidence.

This list demonstrates modality breadth. It is not a corpus quota and does not
assign equal evidentiary strength to unlike sources.

Detailed modality-sensitive research methods belong to PR2-SRC-B. Coverage and
balance across evidence families belong to PR2-CORPUS.

## 7. Functional and generative abstraction

Content-heavy and generator-heavy sources must not be treated as inventories
whose entries imply required Myravant artifacts.

Research must be able to distinguish:

```text
content instance
-> content family
-> generative dimensions
-> generation grammar / coherence relationships
```

A source table with one hundred rows does not create one hundred Myravant
requirements.

A bestiary does not imply renamed Myravant creatures.

An equipment catalog does not imply a Myravant equipment catalog.

An adventure does not imply a Myravant adventure conversion.

A source generator should be studied for the possibility structure, dependency
relationships, coherence constraints, exception patterns, and functions it
demonstrates.

Where a source artifact substitutes for a human role, research should be able to
ask what function the artifact is performing rather than copying its literal
output.

Content-heavy research should increase Myravant's generative understanding
faster than it increases any later canonical catalog.

## 8. Mechanical ecology and utilization preservation

Research must not collapse a rich system into a headline mechanic.

At foundational level, source analysis must preserve enough information for
later method governance to examine relationships among:

- actor types;
- target types;
- activities;
- prerequisites;
- resources;
- consequences;
- other mechanics or institutions;
- changing conditions;
- lifecycle;
- scale;
- environment;
- social context;
- information structure;
- unusual or emergent uses.

Raw entry count is not a valid proxy for structural breadth.

A small set of capabilities with diverse uses may exert broader research
pressure than a large catalog of mechanically repetitive entries.

PR2-SRC-B owns the detailed mechanical-ecology and utilization method.

## 9. Evidence-mode preservation

Research records must preserve enough provenance to distinguish materially
different evidence situations, including the difference between:

- observed implementation;
- published or documented design intent;
- reported user behavior;
- failure or incident evidence;
- empirical findings;
- community reports or hypotheses;
- inferred design rationale;
- abandoned or superseded approaches.

PR2-SRC-A does not freeze a universal confidence scale or evidence taxonomy.

PR2-SRC-B may qualify research methods, and PR2-CORPUS may later own
machine-readable evidence-mode, genealogy, coverage, and bias metadata.

Weak evidence must not become indistinguishable from stronger direct evidence
merely because both produced a pressure record.

## 10. Failure and rules-in-use evidence

Failure is a lawful research input.

Research may derive pressure from:

- broken economies;
- abandoned mechanics;
- exploits and griefing;
- runaway feedback;
- moderation failure;
- scaling failure;
- software incidents;
- AI-agent failures;
- content-generation failure;
- design reversals;
- errata and balance response.

Where evidence permits, research should distinguish designed behavior from
rules-in-use behavior, including the path:

```text
published design
-> actual use
-> emergent strategy
-> exploit or failure
-> correction / errata / redesign
-> long-term consequence
```

A failed approach may provide high-information evidence. Failure does not become
authority; it becomes attributable pressure.

## 11. Ordinary-life and non-adventure pressure

The source architecture must permit research into mundane and institutional
life where relevant to Myravant's long-term possibility space.

Examples include housing, food, sanitation, maintenance, work, education,
childcare, aging, family obligations, leisure, art, religion, illness,
inheritance, administration, and routine social organization.

PR2-SRC-A does not define mechanics for any of these domains.

It prevents the research architecture from becoming structurally limited to
combat, adventuring, exceptional powers, protagonists, or crisis.

## 12. Provenance and source expression

Internal research provenance must remain exact enough for:

- audit;
- reproducibility;
- source-level accountability;
- correction;
- withdrawal;
- later eligibility review;
- later information-barrier enforcement.

Preserving provenance does not grant source expression downstream authority.

PR2-SRC-A complements, and does not replace, the active
`conversion_runtime_origin_firewall_doctrine.md`.

The existing runtime firewall continues to prohibit pre-canon source,
extraction, conversion, mapping, and provenance material from entering runtime.

PR2-IR will later govern the detailed information flow between source-aware
analysis and Myravant-facing design representations.

## 13. Corpus-scale synthesis law

PR2-SRC must assume at least 1,000 heterogeneous sources.

At that scale:

- one source must not imply one Myravant artifact;
- one book must not imply one Myravant book;
- one donor class must not imply one Myravant progression structure;
- one source mechanic must not imply one Myravant mechanic;
- one external software architecture must not imply one Myravant architecture;
- repeated terminology must not become reserved Myravant vocabulary by
  frequency;
- repeated design assumptions must not become doctrine by frequency;
- source prevalence must not be treated as Myravant-world prevalence;
- volume must not substitute for evidence diversity.

PR2-CORPUS later owns registry, coverage, novelty, genealogy, saturation, and
bias control. PR2-SRC-A establishes only the anti-drift laws necessary before
that system exists.

## 14. Lawful research outcomes and escalation

PR2-SRC-A does not adopt the legacy five conversion outcomes as a universal
research-output enum.

Research must instead have a lawful way to produce or route at least:

- attributable observation;
- normalized pressure;
- cross-source synthesis result;
- Myravant-facing requirement;
- outlier finding;
- coverage or evidence gap;
- conflict finding;
- uncertainty requiring deeper research;
- escalation to a lawful downstream owner;
- rejection of an invalid research inference.

If normalization would require decorative Myravant invention, the correct
result is escalation or unresolved research pressure.

If the source does not support the claim, the research record must not silently
fill the gap from general knowledge.

## 15. Downstream handoffs

### PR2-SRC-B — research method qualification

Reserved for:

- modality-sensitive research methods;
- mechanical ecology and utilization method;
- evidence-mode qualification;
- varying research depth;
- source scout versus deep-analysis rules;
- generator/content-heavy analysis method;
- bounded research work-packet requirements.

SRC-A does not authorize SRC-B.

### PR2-SRC-C — legacy source/conversion surface disposition

Reserved for read-only classification of existing extraction, conversion,
donor-mapping, source-pack, and related repository surfaces against the new
research architecture.

SRC-C must classify before remediation. It may not silently rewrite history.

SRC-A does not authorize SRC-C.

### PR2-SRC-D — independent completion review

Reserved for determining whether PR2-SRC as a whole has governed intake,
reconnaissance, pressure extraction, synthesis, provenance handoff, escalation,
method qualification, and legacy disposition sufficiently for successor
workstreams.

The review may not invent missing source doctrine.

SRC-A does not authorize SRC-D.

### PR2-ORG

Owns originality, provenance classification where relevant to eligibility,
rights boundaries, similarity review, and content eligibility.

### PR2-IR

Owns the explicit information barrier and representation contract between
source-aware analysis and Myravant-facing design work.

### PR2-CORPUS

Owns large-scale source registry, batching, coverage, evidence-family balance,
genealogy, novelty, saturation, and corpus-gap governance.

### PR2-FICT

Owns fiction/LitRPG-specific experience and world-behavior pressure rules.

### PR2-SIMEX

Owns simulation/infrastructure exemplar-specific pressure rules.

None of these workstreams is activated by SRC-A.

## 16. Agent and automation boundary

SRC-A may define research outputs that later bounded agents can produce.

It does not authorize autonomous corpus reconnaissance.

Future agent work should be reconstructible from durable external state and
bounded work packets rather than depending on one long-lived agent context.

Deterministic software should remain preferred for hashing, indexing, duplicate
detection, metadata checks, exact comparisons, counts, and schema validation.

High-capability model use should be reserved for bounded semantic judgment.

Detailed agent execution policy is not owned here.

## 17. Anti-drift refusals

PR2-SRC-A rejects the following shortcuts:

- `source -> renamed Myravant content`;
- `source table -> paraphrased Myravant table`;
- `source creature -> renamed Myravant creature`;
- `source ability -> rewritten Myravant ability`;
- `source architecture -> mandatory Myravant architecture`;
- `many sources agree -> therefore Myravant doctrine`;
- `many source entries -> therefore high structural diversity`;
- `source prevalence -> Myravant-world prevalence`;
- `research requirement -> canon`;
- `research requirement -> runtime implementation`;
- `research observation -> live-play authority`;
- `provenance preservation -> permission to expose source lineage at runtime`.

## 18. SRC-A acceptance conditions

PR2-SRC-A is complete only when:

1. the foundational research pipeline and layer distinctions are explicit;
2. external-source nonauthority is explicit;
3. internal provenance continuity is required;
4. source modality neutrality is explicit;
5. functional/generative abstraction is required;
6. cross-source synthesis is the default research-to-requirements route;
7. frequency and prevalence cannot silently create doctrine or world law;
8. failure and rules-in-use evidence have a lawful research route;
9. downstream ownership is explicit and non-overlapping;
10. the existing runtime-origin firewall remains complementary authority;
11. R3 remains independently frozen and unauthorized unless separately
    activated;
12. no source processing, originality adjudication, IR implementation, corpus
    registry, native content, runtime implementation, canon promotion, training,
    or live-play behavior is authorized.
