## Ownership and delivery

AFQR-11/12 retain participation, choice and continued consent; AFQR-03 records
work lifecycle. AFQR-10 retains private evidence; AFQR-14 transfers attributed
assertions, qualified by AFQR-20 sensing and the enumerated communication
profile. AFQR-18/RT-010 own actual location, custody and materials. WORLD-1 and
AFQR-17 own the single clock and passive interval. AFQR-01 owns ordered durable
commitment. No NPC manager acquires those concerns.

The old player default extraction is a separate first commit. New semantic state
is opt-in: actor-indexed participation/due lineage, standing eligibility,
ordered actor receipts, disclosed communication and private evidence. Checkpoints
replay procedures from the qualified root, without trusting a final snapshot.

Preparation selects one fixed intent at interval start. Passive changes, then
player action, then profile-ordered actors requalify it. No same-interval replan
or second physical action follows new observations. Tool recovery is a real
consenting handoff, not withdrawal cleanup. Goal-only choice is tested by bounded
speculative owner qualification without committing that preparation for free.

## Verification observations

### Retained extraction evidence

The Phase 1 isolated worktree contains only the two actor-context extractions
and six compatibility cases. All 17 existing `report_myravant_*.py` runners
passed there, including their installed repeat/restart and mutation controls.
A separate clean baseline archive versus the extraction ran Material/Water/Timed

### Performance sample

The candidate performed 16 separately durable shared intervals with two declared
actors, retaining 1,793 evidence events. Observed local mean interval cost was
0.1484 seconds; full chronological restart cost was 2.4007 seconds. These are
container samples under concurrent verification, not Windows CI predictions.
Campaign stages record launcher and independent verification-restore timings.
The existing 20-minute Linux / 25-minute Windows limits remain unchanged.

### Phase 1 exit

The isolated extraction full suite passed: **10,881 passed, 18 skipped,
2 xfailed, 1 warning in 566.77 seconds**. Skips and expected failures are retained,
not counted as coverage. This certifies the extraction tree
`ba98bba0841cdf8dc1b8302f1049fa90962c317b`; it is preserved in separate commit
`5001fa59096d911ac3058e87e9d0e315f93170d6`. The 17 retained evidence runners
all passed before the feature commit. The warning is the retained pytest fixture
method deprecation, not a gameplay failure.

The final actor candidate additionally passed 85 focused actor tests and 86
manifest/owner/review/mutation controls during integration. Six isolated intended
actor mutations were detected with passing unmutated controls. Separate assessment
executed seven selected variants twice. These integration observations do not
replace the final published-head full suites. Their results and exact CI run
links are maintained in the draft PR description, avoiding a certification-only
source commit that would invalidate the tested head.

After an atomic replacement succeeds but acknowledgement fails, the live session
rolls back and halts. Validated restart can recover the whole newer durable
interval. Before replacement fails, restart retains the earlier acknowledged
prefix. Neither path admits a partial actor/material interval or automatically
repeats offline time.

### Installed campaign integration

Three installed journeys each passed twice with deterministic paired final
states, real material objectives and intermediate restarts: absence/cleanup and
agreed return, contested cooperative assembly, and stale attributed reports.
Bounded two-interval adaptive waiting reduced restart stages from 68 to 50 while
retaining the separate exact-boundary adversaries. Measured runner work fell
from 320.011 to 234.036 seconds (128.520 launcher + 103.800 independent restore
seconds, with small harness overhead). Maximum measured stage launcher/restore
costs were 6.398/6.196 seconds. These are integration samples; CI reports its
own timings at the exact published head.

The full suite exports the already-executed campaigns and separate assessment
for CI artifacts; it does not execute those long journeys again solely to write
reports. All 17 previously retained evidence runners remain enabled. Actor
mutation effectiveness is generated separately. Final review additionally passed
33 targeted cases after correcting operation-local metadata ownership and naming
the original speaker in reported evidence.

### Late sensing regression correction

Final adversarial review reproduced a distinction missed by the initial tests:
carried-object reach remains available in darkness, but it does not admit the
visual goal facets. Goal recognition, standing eligibility and preparation now
use the same light qualification as evidence admission. It is rechecked after
earlier player/actor changes, with an explicit bound task-condition policy.
Two regression cases pass: a carried clean surface grants no dark satisfaction
awareness, and extinguishing a lamp after preparation blocks the actor's scrub
without a second clock, material change or contribution credit. Certification of
the initial published actor head is superseded by this correction's exact-head
runs. Existing player physical affordances and old bindings remain unchanged.

### Public status completion

Local work status includes the actor's qualified blocker/withdrawal reason. The
player's agreement record retains that disclosed reason, so remote queries repeat
only the last communication even if the actual task has since withdrawn. A new
restart regression verifies this distinction and zero-time inquiry. Public
reports identify historical provenance and the original speaker without exposing
backend observation ticks as event dates; those ticks remain in private evidence.
Seventeen targeted status/sensing/knowledge cases passed before final certification.

### Preparation and externally satisfied attribution

A reproduced arrival case distinguished causal completion from new awareness:
an actor returning to an already-clean goal must retain its movement receipt,
but cannot claim completing the cleaning. The task owner can now retain an
actual preparation contribution while recording externally satisfied disposition.
Goal completion requires a newly satisfied predicate from that transition;
external discovery still requires lawful post-action sensing. The regression
failed before correction and passed afterward; 49 related lifecycle, status,
sensing and persistence cases passed. No contributor identity is inferred from
observing the already-satisfied goal.
