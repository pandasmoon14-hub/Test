## Candidate mechanism and authority

WORLD-1 supplies logical time. AFQR-17 supplies declared environmental evolution; RT-010 retains material changes; AFQR-03 records continuing-work lifecycle; existing opportunity/sensing and commitment/persistence owners retain their distinctions. No new general time authority is introduced.

Physical mutations are prequalified, then advance one logical interval. Passive drying uses interval-start placement/exposure and declared rates; the active owner subsequently requalifies. If opportunity disappears during passive evolution, elapsed time remains an executed consequence and the active contribution reports interruption. Metadata declarations and observations consume no interval. Explicit bounded requests are sequences of separately committed intervals, not simultaneous work.

Cleaning and reversible assembly provide two activity-specific continuing-work examples. Their records reference actual material contributions rather than synthetic progress percentages. Waiting does not work on the player's behalf. Pausing, departing, resuming and abandoning preserve already committed material consequences.

Suspended residue remaining after the final water unit becomes a separate deposit rather than disappearing or overflowing ordinary adhered capacity. Neither that compartment nor a drying trace establishes an atmospheric simulation.
