## Player outcome and coherent batch

A player can pursue the same practical goal personally or request a willing local actor's help; the actor chooses and performs qualified methods using their own location, tools and knowledge, continues accepted or independently initiated work during committed world-time advances elsewhere, can be blocked or withdraw, and later reports only what they lawfully did, observed or were told. Contributions, material consequences and actor-specific history survive restart.

One shared actor-context and interval mechanism connects:
1. Scoped voluntary requests, acceptance/refusal and local communication.
2. Two genuine work families: existing cleaning and reversible assembly, including separately qualified preparation and recovery.
3. Two actor-local participation profiles with different declared availability/priorities and real material contention.
4. Bounded standing activities and accepted work advancing on the existing world clock, including player absence.
5. Narrow witnessed/reported activity evidence and lawful later status.
6. Complete mixed-owner receipts, interval durability and replay.

This is more than giving two actors names or adding commands. Actor position, actual custody, willingness, competing commitments, accessible supplies and lawful evidence must change executable outcomes.

## 1. Participation and communication

Admit typed proposals for a named actor, goal, explicit method where supplied, participants and permitted task scope. At least these decisions must be observable through appropriate public feedback: accepted, declined, busy, unavailable, unsupported/needs clarification, and later blocked/withdrawn/completed.

- Requesting is not controlling. A local request must pass current communication opportunity, actor participation policy and target/method scope.
- Show truthful acceptance, not premature success. Accepted work creates an attributed task/participation record; no object is moved, cleaned or fastened by acceptance alone.
- Proposed timing: as with existing work declarations, request acceptance and accepted task-control bookkeeping consume **zero logical intervals** and are separately receipted/durable. Rejected requests change no authoritative state. They do not advance NPC routines. Observation/status also consumes no time. This is an explicit development timing policy for the new binding, not universal speech speed.
- Newly accepted work first becomes due during a **subsequent committed interval**; never perform it retroactively in the tick that preceded acceptance. Bind eligibility to that interval's identity and the accepted participation record, not merely a numeric clock comparison. Repeated zero-time accept/cancel/control attempts cannot enqueue duplicate participation, apply a prior interval or execute work.
- **Admitted communication route for this binding:** both participants are co-located at the same qualified place, the addressed actor is currently perceptible under sufficient local light, and no closed/blocked access boundary prevents the interaction. The participation profile explicitly admits the addressed request/status/report class. Same parent-area label, a directional sightline, a known name or a prior conversation is insufficient. Adjacent/remote, through-closed-boundary, dark/unperceived, signed-only, written and mediated channels are unavailable unless separately and explicitly admitted; this batch does not invent hearing or universal speech physics. The light requirement is a conservative development qualification, not a claim that people universally cannot communicate in darkness. Actor-to-actor communication uses the same qualification, not a privileged background channel. Remote status never exposes hidden actor activity.
- Each actor has at most one nonterminal selected task. Busy actors decline another incompatible job; no speculative queue/reservation system.
- Before every due physical step, revalidate actor-local continued consent, scope and eligibility against the current ordered state, including changes from earlier player/NPC steps. A withdrawn/blocked prepared intent leaves that action slot unused; it cannot select and execute a replacement standing activity in the same interval.
- **Proposed default continuing-participation policy:** an accepted task withdraws when its consent/scope becomes invalid, or after **K = 3 consecutive blocked due intervals**. K is a positive bounded integer in the immutable actor/task profile, not a universal patience statistic. Each actually due committed interval counts once if the actor cannot perform its selected contribution; a successful physical preparation/contribution resets the counter. Explicitly paused or not-yet-due intervals, zero-time requests/observations, and technical retries do not increment it. A blocked task is requalified once on each otherwise due interval, so the policy cannot stall forever merely because its lifecycle is labeled blocked. Qualified observation that the objective is already satisfied resolves the corresponding disposition rather than consuming a fake contribution or triggering patience withdrawal. Consent invalidation is checked before any owner transition and prevents action in that interval; timeout withdrawal occurs at the Kth blocked due boundary. Both counter changes and withdrawal decisions are receipted, persisted and replayed. Withdrawal does not return tools, erase work or refresh remote status by magic. Renewing withdrawn participation requires new consent; it cannot resurrect prior contributions as new work. Acceptance variants must change K and exercise K−1/K/K+1 boundaries across restart. Player cancellation remains a proposal the actor may accept, not a universal control grant; the actor's own standing work is not automatically cancellable by the player.
- A completed or abandoned participation record preserves actual contributions and physical changes. Do not infer wages, debt, ownership, promises or institutional obligations from accepting a bounded task.

Accepted request/task status can be inspectable as the player's own agreement record; it must be labeled **last communicated status**, not a live remote view of the performer.

## 2. Goals, methods and real preparation

Reuse existing procedures for cleaning and reversible assembly:
- Cleaning: explicit wipe/scrub or goal-only cleaning with a profile-declared choice among currently qualified methods. Wet cloth, brush capacity, water, deposits, rinse/collection and real remaining residue matter.
- Assembly: support placement and declared fastening, with actual materials and separate recovery. This is not East Gate repair, fabrication, structural load or universal tying.

An explicitly requested method may not silently change. A goal-only request may choose from its declared alternatives; choice and eventual performance must be distinguishable. Unsupported modifiers must be preserved/refused, not discarded to manufacture success.

Preparation uses real steps: declared one-edge movement, pickup/handoff, retrieve/open where authorized, soak/rinse/collection, or support placement. **At most one physical owner transition per actor per interval**, including preparation. No automatic gathering bundle, teleportation, free tool cleaning or infinite resupply. Route plans must be explicit bounded declared paths; a blocked route pauses/blocks actual progress, not general pathfinding or bypass creation.

Every transition requalifies the performing actor's location, custody, capabilities and opportunity. A role label, mentorship or engineering willingness policy supplies no missing embodiment, tool qualification, private access or canonical authority.

Two distinct methods for one goal must lead to meaningfully different lawful prerequisites/consequences, not just synonyms.

## 3. Actor-local work, self-initiated activity and resource contention

Retain one task lifecycle per actor using existing intention distinctions, with actor-indexed selection, real goal/method/participants/place, accepted scope, blockers and contribution receipts. The existing shared world remains authoritative; task counters cannot substitute for changed materials.

Each of the two admitted actor profiles must demonstrate at least one standing development activity selected from its own declared goal/availability without a fresh player command. Demonstration standing activities use the same cleaning/assembly families, with explicit root eligibility/due conditions. Completion stops satisfied work; do not repeatedly re-soil/reset the target to manufacture a loop. These are bounded activities, not an entire daily-life simulation or canonical timetable.

- Player and NPC contributions may affect the same material goal. Preserve who actually performed each step. If another actor already satisfies a task goal, record an externally satisfied disposition/evidence link rather than falsely granting this actor a contribution/completion credit. Task adjudication may establish the physical goal predicate, but the actor may discover or report satisfaction only through qualified observation or communication during task revalidation; backend goal truth, the actor's awareness and the player's last communicated status remain separate. A remote change alone must not magically inform the actor or the player.
- Current physical custody/availability handles initial contention. Accepting a task does not reserve or transfer the brush, cloth, fastener or water.
- Two actors competing for the same tool/supply cannot both use it from stale snapshots. Requalify after earlier commits. A loser can report unavailable/blocked or choose an allowed alternative on the next interval.
- Persist blocked/paused reasons with lawful public projection. Do not leak another actor's hidden possessions or private goal as an explanatory convenience.
- No infinite same-tick retry or replan. No starvation/fairness guarantee is claimed by deterministic ordering; revise policy only on demonstrated play pressure and explicit profile/version change.
- Do not add reservation, currency, employment or generalized inventory promise primitives merely to implement initial custody contention.

## 4. One world interval, multiple qualified actors

Preserve old timing in old bindings. Add a separate opt-in actor-aware binding/version.

Recommended deterministic order for each admitted physical player action/work interval or bounded wait:
1. At t, prequalify the requested player attempt (or wait) and select eligible NPC intents from each actor's permitted state/evidence. An initial player refusal advances nothing.
2. Advance existing WORLD-1 logical time exactly once to t+1.
3. Apply passive environmental effects from actual interval-start exposure; scheduled boundary conditions apply to following intervals, as in #546.
4. Requalify/resolve the player's active contribution, if any, retaining honest executed interruption when passive changes remove its opportunity.
5. Requalify/resolve at most one prepared contribution for each due NPC in the **explicit profile-bound stable actor order**. This is an ordered development resolution policy, not simultaneous physical law. Player priority and NPC order are declared, persisted and tested.
6. Admit narrowly qualified actor observations of the actual ordered changes, recording causal source and sensing evidence. Observation admission can follow each committed subevent so witnesses are judged at the event's actual context; newly learned facts cannot cause a second physical action in that interval.
7. Validate and durably commit the complete interval, its ordered subevents and cross-owner receipts before acknowledging any success.

NPC transitions must use **clock-free owner execution** within this shared interval. Never call public timed methods recursively so each NPC adds another tick or reschedules the whole world.

A contextual NPC failure blocks that contribution while preserving the lawful clock/passive/player/other-actor effects in the completed interval. An invariant failure or failed durable write rolls back the **whole unacknowledged interval**; no partially acknowledged actor history. Preserve earlier acknowledged intervals of a bounded duration/sequence. Refusal is not an environmental time machine.

Use immutable prepared state and explicit actor contexts, with canonical traversal/order and versioned policy witnesses. A technical retry must not repeat time, actions, observations or contribution receipts; deliberately repeated player attempts remain distinct.

## 5. Player absence, routines and qualified knowledge

All admitted actors remain individual persistent identities. Accepted/self-initiated eligible work can advance while the player is at another admitted local position, but only when world time actually advances. Player absence is neither a pause command nor a permission grant.

No real-time/offline catch-up while the application is closed. Restore applies committed elapsed history once. No region aggregation/materialization, infinite region simulation or new fidelity substrate is needed for two admitted actors.

Narrow knowledge extension:
- A performer can retain its own committed activity/result.
- A witness records only profile-declared observations qualified by that actor's actual position, light, closure and sensing opportunity. **The player-actor uses this same actor-generic admission mechanism as NPCs** for declared witnessed records; there is no player-only omniscient evidence path. Witness qualification is actor-specific even if two people are in the same place. Existing legacy player output does not silently acquire new memory semantics; this symmetry applies in the new binding. Player-visible text may project only that player's admitted evidence, not the private witness stores of others.
- A communicated assertion records its speaker and reported provenance; it is not automatically first-hand knowledge or world truth.
- A stale observation remains historical. The actor cannot consult current world placement merely to “refresh” an answer. A false player report does not change object location or grant hidden sensing.
- Local later inquiry may report the actor's performed steps, current declared task disposition, last witnessed facts or attributed report, with uncertainty/unknown/unsupported distinguished.
- No automatic belief engine, truth inference, accusation, exact event dating, universal testimony or omniscient private-goal dump. If an established canonical fact lies outside implemented inquiry scope, say that inquiry is unsupported rather than inventing that the person knows nothing.
- Do not disclose the hidden Pin Blank location, sealed packet contents, private household access or another actor's private memory merely from runtime provenance. Office, custody, communication and evidence remain distinct.

Player-facing text should say what happened or why the available task cannot proceed, in understandable terms. Internal formulas, scheduling machinery and backend IDs belong in developer evidence unless practically relevant.

## 7. Combined acceptance matrix

1. **Choice and consent:** accept/refuse/busy/absent; explicit method retained; goal-only alternatives selected from current lawful opportunities; withdrawal/revocation while partial consequences persist.
2. **Two activities/two actors:** both work families demonstrated; actor capability/availability variations change lawful outcomes. Role names do not select methods in shared execution.
3. **Real performer context:** player can lack the tool/light/access that the NPC lawfully has and vice versa; no borrowed player senses/custody. Receipts attribute the actual actor.
4. **Contention:** player/NPC and NPC/NPC compete for finite tools/fasteners/water; both actor-order variants; no duplicate custody/use; no acceptance-created reservation; no stale stock.
5. **Continuing consequences:** pause, interruption, changed prerequisites, resume, abandon, completion, externally satisfied goal, return/recovery. Another actor's contribution never becomes this actor's work.
6. **One clock:** 0/1/2 active NPCs still yield exactly one world tick per interval; nested movement/material/water cannot multiply time; observation and request metadata do not tick; bounded waits equal single-step histories.
7. **Passive interaction:** last water dries before a contribution; closed containment pauses; actor move affects next interval exposure; passive/NPC/player ordering has exact causal receipts.
8. **Absence:** leave, advance time elsewhere, return; blocked standing work remains blocked rather than teleporting resources; restart does not apply elapsed work twice.
9. **Knowledge/privacy:** observer-specific light/closure/locality; witnessed versus reported/false/stale assertions; no remote live status, inferred culprit or private-world truth leakage.
10. **Generality:** neutral identities/topology and at least two configuration-only participation/priority variants using the same actor-qualified procedures; do not substitute constant monkeypatching for transfer evidence.
11. **Failure depth:** whole-interval rollback before durable acknowledgement; prefix survival after later duration failure; failure around atomic replacement; malformed/resealed task/actor/consent/schedule/evidence/deposit/ordering forgery rejected.
12. **Effectiveness:** isolated negative controls disable consent, performer qualification, single-tick composition, contention requalification, evidence privacy and full replay attribution; each intended defect is detected with passing controls.
13. **Installed play:** several objective-first adaptive journeys, each twice with intermediate restarts; e.g. tool supply/cleaning while absent, contested preparation/barrier work and changed method opportunity, stale/report versus witnessed evidence followed by lawful action. Keep held-out assessor goals separate from implementation-authored transcripts; human acceptance remains deferred.
14. **Regression and measurement:** original corpora/old bindings retained; exact-head full local/Linux/Windows checks and retained evidence pipeline; measure per-interval/restart cost and existing 20/25-minute CI headroom before any budget change.
